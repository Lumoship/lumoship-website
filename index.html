What we're building

Three pillars of structural engineering software

Some tools are live today, others are in active development. We're building toward a complete workflow, one capability at a time.

Structural Analysis

Beam-based analysis for ship structures.

Grillage solver for hatch covers, decks, bulkheads, and stiffened panels. Built on classical Timoshenko beam theory with marine-grade materials and multi-load case support.

In active development · Beta Q2 2026

Rule-Based Design

Class society compliance and scantling calculations.

Midship section design tools, scantling check modules, and rule-based calculations for DNV, LR, BV, and RINA. Plus a library of supporting utilities for everyday engineering work.

First tools live · Suite expanding through 2026

Parametric Modeling

Rapid structural modeling, integrated with CAD.

A Rhino-integrated plugin for parametric hull and structural modeling — generate analysis-ready models in minutes, not days. The bridge between geometry and analysis.

Planned · 2026 roadmap

Driven by feedback from naval architects, design offices, and shipyards.
Tool priorities reflect real industry demand.
