About Lumoship

Building modern engineering software for the marine industry.

Vision

Engineering tools, reimagined for the modern era

Lumoship was founded to bring modern software practices to marine structural engineering. The maritime industry runs on decades-old workflows, custom Excel spreadsheets, and €30,000-per-year software licenses that small design offices cannot afford.

We believe engineers deserve better. Browser-based tools that work instantly. Class-compliant calculations they can trust. Fair pricing for individual practitioners and small teams. And software built by people who understand the domain.

What we're building

Three pillars of structural engineering

Our roadmap is organized around three core disciplines — each addressing a real bottleneck in modern ship design workflows.

01

Structural Analysis

Beam-based analysis for ship structures. Grillage solver for hatch covers, decks, bulkheads, and stiffened panels — built on classical Timoshenko beam theory.

02

Rule-Based Design

Class society compliance and scantling calculations. Midship section design tools, scantling check modules, and rule-based calculations for DNV, LR, BV, and RINA.

03

Parametric Modeling

Rapid structural modeling, integrated with CAD. A Rhino-integrated plugin for parametric hull and structural modeling — the bridge between geometry and analysis.
